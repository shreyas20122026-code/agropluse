Sample Shivamogga Crop Pathologies:
ARECANUT (BETEL NUT)

Koleroga / Fruit Rot (Phytophthora meadii)

98.2%
Symptoms & Cause:

Water-soaked dark lesions on the nut base and calyx caused by Phytophthora meadii. Favored by continuous heavy Malnad monsoon rainfall, high humidity (>90%), and cool temperatures (20-23°C).

Treatment & Cure:

Chemical: Spray 1% Bordeaux mixture to all bunches or apply Metalaxyl + Mancozeb (Ridomil MZ @ 2g/L).

Organic: Cover bunches with polythene covers (Arecanut bunch bagging); apply Pseudomonas fluorescens foliar spray.

Preventive Farm SOP:

Collect and burn all dropped infected nuts from plantation floor. Ensure good field drainage in red laterite soil to avoid stagnant water basins.
